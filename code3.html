Turning Tokens into Numbers

Computers can't do math with words, so every token gets its own ID number. The list of all known tokens and their numbers is called a vocabulary. After this step, your sentence is just a list of numbers. That's all the chatbot ever "reads"!

Your mission: Replace pass with ids.append(vocab[token]) and click Run. Then change text to your own sentence. Notice how repeated words get the same number!

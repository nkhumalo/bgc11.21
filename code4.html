How Chatbots "Write": Guessing the Next Word

Here's the big secret: chatbots don't plan out what they want to say. They look at the words so far and guess what word probably comes next, one word at a time. This mini chatbot learns which words follow which, then writes by guessing. Real chatbots do the same thing with billions of words and much fancier math.

Your mission: Add at least two of your own sentences to training_text and click Run a few times. Put a space before each period, like i love stuffing . How does the chatbot's writing change with your new data?

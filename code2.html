Chopping Text into Tokens

AI chatbots don't read whole sentences the way you do. The first thing they do is chop text into small pieces called tokens. Real chatbots split long words into pieces, like "chatbots" becoming "chat" + "bots." We'll keep it simple and use whole words.

Your mission: Replace tokens = [] with tokens = clean.split() and click Run. Then change sentence to something you would say and run it again. How many tokens did you get?
